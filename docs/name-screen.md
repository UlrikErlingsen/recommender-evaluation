# Working-name screen: RecommendSignal

Screen date: 16 July 2026.

## Result

**RecommendSignal is a plausible internal working name, but it is not legally cleared.** Exact-phrase web, GitHub-index, and package-registry searches did not surface an obvious active recommendation-software product using the exact combined name during this basic screen. The exact PyPI project URL returned no project.

Search results did surface “recommend signal” as ordinary language, including a research paper about recommending traffic-signal plans. Searches involving the two separate words are also noisy because “recommend” is generic and Signal is a prominent messaging product and common technical term. This creates some search and distinctiveness friction even without an obvious exact-name collision.

## Practical assessment

- **Direct exact-name conflict found:** none obvious in the basic web screen.
- **Conceptual fit:** strong for an evidence tool that evaluates recommendation signals and policies.
- **Discoverability:** moderate; both component words are common.
- **Legal confidence:** low, because no formal trademark clearance was performed.

## Limits

This was a practical product-name screen, not legal advice or a trademark opinion. It did not exhaust official trademark registers, national company registers, common-law rights, domains and historical ownership, app stores, social handles, translations, or every relevant class and jurisdiction. Search absence is not proof of availability.

## Recommendation

Retain **RecommendSignal** as the requested working title. Before public or commercial launch, check exact and similar spellings in the relevant official trademark and company registers, confirm domain and social-handle strategy, repeat GitHub/PyPI/npm/app-store searches, and obtain qualified legal advice if the name will carry material value.
