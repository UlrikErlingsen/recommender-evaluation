# Privacy

RecommendSignal is local-first and implements no telemetry, accounts, advertising, or built-in third-party upload. Uploaded files are processed in the active Streamlit process.

Recommendation histories, inferred profiles, subgroups, and slates can expose sensitive interests. Use pseudonymous identifiers, minimize fields, restrict user-level exports, define retention, and apply appropriate legal and organizational controls. Hosting providers may add logs or persistence outside this codebase; deployment operators are responsible for reviewing them.
